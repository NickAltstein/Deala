01 / STATE

Commercial Recall

Persist intent, constraints, evidence dependencies and decisions. Re-evaluate only when a supporting condition changes.

02 / AUTHORITY

Operating Mandate

Scope reads and proposed actions outside model reasoning. Missing authority, stale approvals and unresolved material conflicts block the next move.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Signed evidence

Bind records to verifiable keys and reconcile each event to an explicit disposition. A valid signature is not proof of factual correctness.

MARKET / TICKET SIZE

High-value inventory

Five- and six-figure transactions can make a single recovered opportunity material. The $13,450 example is illustrative, not a market average.

MARKET / CHANNELS

Across channels.

Buying requirements can sit across authorized message histories, SMS and dealer systems, disconnected from later inventory events.

MARKET / RECALL GAP

Manual follow-up.

Where workflows depend on manual logging and human recall, changed conditions may never reach the original decision.
