What it verifies

SHA-256 over the serialized subject and Ed25519 signature against the embedded public key. Legacy demo v1 uses trade_record; v2 includes subject.type in the signed object.

What it does not

No trusted signer identity, authorization, judgment, timestamp or production integrity is established. Envelope metadata is unsigned. This is not a production receipt verifier.
